Connect Telegram

{{ notifyStepLabel }}

First, create your own personal bot — it takes about a minute.

  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather
  2. Send the message /newbot
  3. Give it any name and username it asks for
  4. BotFather replies with a long token — copy it

Now find your Chat ID — this tells the bot who to message.

  1. Open a chat with the bot you just created
  2. Send it any message, like "hi"
  3. It replies instantly with your Chat ID

All set. Orbit will send your reminders through your own Telegram bot.
